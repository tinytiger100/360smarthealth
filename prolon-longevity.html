The Prolon fasting mimicking diet for weight loss and longevity

Fasting can be beneficial to your health. I also tell my patients that if it is not done properly, it can be detrimental. In my clinical experience, a fast that is not done carefully can release a large amount of stored toxins into the bloodstream. It can also cause a significant loss of muscle. That muscle is very hard to replace, and building muscle is critical as you age.

Prolon is the fasting plan I recommend. It is a scientific fasting-mimicking diet, which means it is designed to make your body think it is fasting without actually fasting.1,2 You still eat. It is a 5-day, plant-based meal program from L-Nutra,2,3 developed from research led by Dr. Valter Longo at the University of Southern California.1,2 The published menu is low in calories and protein: vegetable-based soups, energy bars, drinks, snacks, tea, and a micronutrient supplement, eaten for 5 days in a row rather than water only.1,2 The small amount of nutrients in the Prolon meal plan is small enough to keep your body thinking it is fasting, yet it provides enough nutrients that you don't feel like you are fasting.

When I talk with patients about autophagy, I describe it as the body’s cleanup. It clears old, damaged, senescent cells that can reduce metabolic efficiency. Research on prolonged fasting has linked that kind of fast with autophagy, and has also noted that a water-only fast is difficult for most people and can be too extreme. The fasting-mimicking diet was developed to seek those fasting-like effects while you still eat.1 In a randomized trial, generally healthy adults who finished this 5-day plan once a month for 3 months had lower body weight, trunk and total body fat, blood pressure, and IGF-1, a growth factor the study tracked, and no serious adverse effects were reported.2 A 2024 analysis of fasting-mimicking diet trial samples, using the L-Nutra program boxed as Prolon, found that 3 cycles were associated with a 2.5-year decrease in median biological age, independent of weight loss.3

In short, the Prolon fasting mimicking diet allows you to eat specific foods and nutrients during a period in which your body thinks it is fasting, which makes it a comfortable way to enjoy the health benefits fasting can promote. It is one of the most scientifically studied diets, has numerous patents, and allows you to get the benefits of fasting without damaging your body. In my view, mitochondrial health is the key to health and longevity, and this diet helps create mitochondrial health.

Questions about the discount and who should skip it

How do I get the discount?

Click the green button. The 5-20% provider discount is applied through my provider link.

Who should not do Prolon?

Prolon says not to use the 5-day plan if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, under 18 or over 70, have a BMI under 18.5, have an eating disorder, have an active infection, are malnourished or protein deficient, or have specific dietary restrictions. People with diabetes who use insulin or another glucose-lowering medicine should not start it on their own. If you take medication or have a medical condition, check with your physician first.

How often should it be done?

Prolon's guidance is three cycles, about a month apart, then three to four cycles a year, and not more than once a month. More frequent use has not been tested. Ask your physician what is right for you.
